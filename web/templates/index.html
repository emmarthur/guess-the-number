About this app

A classic number-guessing game. The app picks a secret integer between {{ min_number }} and {{ max_number }}. You narrow it down using higher/lower feedback until you guess correctly or use all {{ max_guesses }} attempts.

How to play

  • Enter a whole number in the box and press Guess.
  • Last hint shows whether your guess was too low, too high, or correct.
  • Watch Guesses left — when it hits zero, the secret number is revealed.
  • Use Play again to start a fresh round with a new secret number.
